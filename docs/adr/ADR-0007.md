# ADR-0007: Scheduled delivery snapshot and rule mutation

Status: **Accepted** — 2026-07-25
Implemented: **2026-08-01, Prompt 7** — see [ADR-0015](ADR-0015.md), which records
the decisions this one deliberately left open: where the snapshot lives and when
it is released, when the identity is claimed, the six execution-time checks and
their distinct reasons, the two cancellation directions, and the Action Scheduler
contract.

## Context

Deliveries can be **delayed** (send N hours after the trigger). Between
scheduling and execution the store owner may edit the rule, disable it, delete
it, or the order may change (be refunded, cancelled, or have items removed).
If a delayed delivery read the *live* rule at execution time, an edit would
silently mutate already-queued mail, and a delete would either error or send
stale content. We need a delivery that is **decided at scheduling time** and
whose content is **immune to later rule edits**, while its *personal data* is
still **fresh** at send time.

## Decision

### Snapshot at scheduling time

When a delayed delivery is scheduled, the claim row snapshots:

- rule **revision**,
- **subject / heading / body templates**,
- **recipient definitions**,
- **matched item IDs**,
- **mode**,
- **trigger identity**.

The snapshot contains **no rendered personal data** — it stores *templates and
definitions*, not resolved customer content.

### Cancellation on disable/delete

**Disabling or deleting a rule immediately cancels its pending scheduled
deliveries**, and the cancellation is **logged with a reason**
([ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md): `disabled-before-scheduled-execution` /
`cancelled`).

### Execution-time validation

At execution, before sending, the delivery verifies:

1. the **rule still exists and is enabled**;
2. the **order is not cancelled / refunded / failed**;
3. **at least one snapshotted matched item still exists and is not fully
   refunded**.

If any check fails, the delivery is **cancelled with a reason**
(`invalidated-by-order-state` / `cancelled`) and no mail is sent.

### Fresh placeholders, snapshot content

At send time:

- **Placeholders resolve fresh from the live order** (current customer name,
  totals, addresses, etc.).
- **Content comes from the snapshot** (the subject/heading/body templates
  captured at scheduling time).
- The delivery **records the revision that produced it** (audit column,
  [ADR-0004](ADR-0004.md)).

### Editing never re-arms

**Editing a rule never mutates or re-arms queued or past deliveries.** A queued
delivery keeps its snapshot; a past delivery is immutable. A rule edit only
affects deliveries scheduled *after* the edit.

## Consequences

- A store owner editing a rule cannot accidentally change the content of mail
  already queued to specific customers; queued mail carries the content that was
  live when it was scheduled.
- Disabling or deleting a rule is an effective, immediate stop for its pending
  mail, and the reason is auditable.
- Customers never receive mail for an order that was cancelled/refunded, or for
  items that were fully refunded before the delayed send, because execution-time
  validation cancels those deliveries.
- Personal data is never stored rendered in the queue (privacy-minimal); it is
  resolved fresh at send, so a customer who changed their name/address between
  scheduling and send gets correct data, while the *message* stays as authored.
- The recorded revision ties every sent delivery back to the exact rule version
  that produced it, without that revision ever influencing idempotency
  ([ADR-0004](ADR-0004.md) keeps revision out of the uniqueness key).
- Because editing does not re-arm, there is no interaction where a rule change
  causes a re-send of an already-delivered identity.
